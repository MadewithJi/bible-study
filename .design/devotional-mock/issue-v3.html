Loved first

John 3 · Romans 5 · 1 John 4  ·  6 chapters, 4 notes, 3 connections

Gustave Doré, The Crucifixion (1866)

I spent most of this week in John 3, and kept being pulled back to one word: gave. Before anything is asked of us, something is given. By Friday the same thread had run through Romans 5 and 1 John 4, so this week’s notes are really about one thing.

John, on why the Son was sent:

“

For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son,

that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life.”

John 3:16 · BSB

I · John 3

The giving comes first

I have read this verse my whole life and never slowed down on the order of it. Loved, then gave, then believes. The believing comes third. Everything I do is a response to something already done.

“For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through Him.”

John 3:17 · BSB

My highlight · John 3:17

but to save the world through Him — the sending was for rescue, not for a verdict. The direction runs toward us first.

II · Romans 5

While we were still

“But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”

Romans 5:8 · BSB

“While we were still.” Not after we had cleaned up. The same order as John 3: the gift lands in the middle of the mess, not at the end of it. I linked this one back to John 3:16, and it is hard to read either without the other now.

This week’s connections

One thread, four books

The verses this week linked, drawn across the whole Bible, Genesis to Revelation.

Gustave Doré, The Prophet Isaiah (1866)

“But He was pierced for our transgressions, He was crushed for our iniquities; the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him, and by His stripes we are healed.”

Isaiah 53:5 · BSB

III · 1 John 4

Because

“And love consists in this: not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son as the atoning sacrifice for our sins.”

1 John 4:10 · BSB

Six words in verse 19 settle the whole week. The “because” carries all of it. My love for God, for my family, for the people who are hard to love, is not the source. It is the echo.

For today

If you take one thing from this week, take the order: loved, given, believed, and only then, loving. It is a relief to find myself third on that list.

“We love because He first loved us.”

1 John 4:19 · BSB
Amen.
