Week 1

Loved first

8–14 November 2026 · John 3, Romans 5, 1 John 4

I spent most of this week in John 3, and kept being pulled back to one word: gave. Before anything is asked of us, something is given. By Friday the same thread had run through Romans 5 and 1 John 4, so this week’s notes are really about one thing.

John 3 · The giving comes first

For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life.

John 3:16 · BSB

I have read this verse my whole life and never slowed down on the order of it. Loved, then gave, then believes. The believing comes third. Everything I do is a response to something already done.

For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through Him.

John 3:17 · BSB

Highlighted: not that we loved God, but that He loved us — my note on it: the direction matters. It runs toward us first.

Romans 5 · While we were still

But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

Romans 5:8 · BSB

“While we were still.” Not after we had cleaned up. The same order as John 3: the gift lands in the middle of the mess, not at the end of it. I linked this one back to John 3:16 as same thought, and it is hard to read either without the other now.

→ John 3:16 · same thought · the gift precedes the response

→ Isaiah 53:5 · fulfils · what the giving cost

1 John 4 · Because

We love because He first loved us.

1 John 4:19 · BSB

Six words that settle the whole week. The “because” carries all of it. My love for God, for my family, for the people who are hard to love, is not the source. It is the echo.

If you take one thing from this week, take the order: loved, given, believed, and only then, loving. It is a relief to find myself third on that list.
